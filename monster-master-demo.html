Monster Master

A recreation of the Miniplay card game — starring the Embark plushies
How to play
  • Reduce the rival to 0 HP to win. You both start at 20 HP.
  • Each turn you draw a card. Play one monster per turn — and as many spells as you like.
  • Monsters take a few turns to summon (shown on the card). While summoning they can't attack.
  • Battles are dice rolls. Tap one of your ready monsters, then tap a rival monster. The attacker rolls ATK + a 6-sided die, the defender rolls DEF + a 6-sided die. Whoever rolls higher wins — the loser takes damage equal to the difference. Equal rolls: no damage. If the rival has no monsters, your monster hits the rival directly for its ATK. Each of your ready monsters can attack once per turn. The rolls and the result show in the Last battle box.
  • Poison: Bog Frog and Ink Squid poison the loser whenever they win a battle, attacking or defending. A poisoned monster takes 2 damage at the start of its owner's next 2 turns. Mist Unicorn is immune to poison.
  • Monster abilities: Void Invader has Life Drain (heals by the damage it deals when it wins a battle). Kernal Acorn has Thorns (deals 1 damage back even when it loses). Pond Turtle is a Guardian (the rival must attack it first). Atlas has Rally (your other monsters gain +1 ATK when it enters). Dragon Lord is immune to spells.
  • Combine: monsters with the same symbol can fuse into a super monster. The symbol is the monster's rarity (the border color). Play a Combine card, then tap two of your monsters with the same symbol — they merge into a super monster with boosted ATK/DEF and their HP added together, ready to attack right away.
  • Spells: Fireball (3 damage to a monster), Lightning Strike (2 damage to an enemy monster), Swamp (1 damage to every monster), Sword (one of your monsters +2 ATK), Weaken (an enemy monster −3 ATK), Curse (an enemy monster −1 ATK and −1 DEF), Sacrifice (destroy one of your monsters to heal 4 HP), Heal (+4 HP to one of your monsters), Poison (a monster takes 2 damage for 2 turns), Combine (fuse two same-symbol monsters), Shield (one of your monsters +2 DEF), Freeze (an enemy monster can't attack next turn), Revive (bring your strongest destroyed monster back at half HP), Meteor (2 damage to all enemy monsters), Haste (your summoning monsters finish 1 turn sooner), Double Strike (one of your monsters attacks twice this turn), Blessing (heal your own HP by 4).
  • Tap End Turn when you're done. The rival plays, then it's your turn again.
  • Build your own deck: before a game, pick 30 cards (max 2 copies of each type). The rival plays with the same deck, shuffled separately. Or tap Random Deck for a surprise build, or Use Default Deck for the full 49-card deck.
