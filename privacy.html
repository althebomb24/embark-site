Embark — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Embark ("the app") is a goal and habit companion that turns real-life goals into story quests. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the app stores

All of your game progress — goals, quest progress, XP, journal entries, streaks, and collections — is stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts, and we do not operate a server that receives or stores your progress or journal content.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information. We do not track your location. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics. We do not sell personal data to anyone.

Purchases

Optional story packs and the Premium subscription are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed by Apple or Google — we never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and unlock content; RevenueCat receives a purchase receipt and an anonymous app-generated identifier, never your journal entries or personal information. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Content downloads

The app downloads story content (story packs) from our content host. Like any web request, the host's standard server logs may record your device's IP address and the file requested. We do not use these logs to identify or track users.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also reset all progress from within the app's profile screen at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
