How it plays

  • Race: 20 turns. First to distance 100 wins. If nobody finishes by turn 20, whoever is furthest wins. If a ship's hull hits 0, it's destroyed and loses. Both ships are pirate ships — base speed 5, same as each other.
  • Draft: pick 5 crew from a pool of 10 before the race. The range is wide — 5★ specialists who are 1★ at everything else, steady 3★ all-rounders, and a 2★ Flex. Your crew decides how fast tasks go and how much missile warning you get.
  • Commands: 5 commands per turn. A task costs 1 more command than the turns it takes (a 1-turn task costs 2 commands, a 4-turn task costs 5). The Flex's Boost is just 1 command (but it still takes 3 turns to finish). Use all 5 or just most — tap End turn to resolve. Only 6 task options show per turn (the most useful ones for the situation), plus the free one-time actions. Instant actions (power-up, sacrifice) are free. Cheer costs 1 command.
  • Crew: each crew member has an overall rating (big stars), then the initials of their other roles with a star level each (e.g. Pilot · E1 · G2 · R2). Pick who does a task — each task shows a few crew members who can do it, at different costs. Tasks take fewer turns with more stars (5★ = 1 turn, 4★/3★ = 2, 2★ = 3, 1★ = 4; a Flex takes 3 everywhere). A Flex holds progress when reassigned, and their Cheer costs 1 command (+1 speed this turn, once per turn).
  • Missiles (pick the right one): Dart (1 command, 1 damage — cheap, use it with your last command), Standard cannon (damage scales with gunner stars), Salvo (damage +2, shoots down a missile too), Torpedo (double damage, costs more — best on a healthy rival). Only Standard and Salvo shoot down an incoming missile.
  • The rival: its ship is faster than yours (base speed 7 vs your 5), so you can't win by just shooting — you have to boost too. It only repairs when its hull is 6 or less (+2), so your damage actually sticks now.
  • Don't spam one system: using the same system 4 times in a row (engines, cannons, sensors, or the repair bay) breaks it — an engineer has to fix it before you can use it again. Vary your actions.
  • Damage slows a ship: when a ship's hull drops to 8, 5, or 2, its movement falls off by 1 each time (base 5, both ships). Hit the rival to slow it down.
  • Recon stars = warning. A missile is incoming in your best recon's stars turns (5 with Ink Squid, 4 with Mist Unicorn, +1 after a Scan). The rival launches one every 3 turns (turns 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18). Shoot it down (gunner), dodge it (evasive maneuvers), jam it (recon), or focus shields to absorb it. If it hits: hull −6 and you're slowed.
  • Halfway (turn 10): a random hazard — asteroid field or space pirates. Pick an approach: evade, ram through with shields, or have recon prepare in advance. Each uses a different crew member.
  • Power-up (once per race): All-Out Cannon Blast — a missile barrage the rival can't fully stop. It triages and limits the damage, so it's a big hit, not a win button.
  • Sacrifice: dump the cannons for +1 speed permanently — but you lose cannon fire for the rest of the race. Available from the halfway mark (turn 10).
  • Crew suggestions: every few seconds a crew member suggests something. Tap it to do it, if you can afford it.
  • Log: what the rival ship does shows in bold red, including how far it moved each turn — so you can see its moves at a glance.

Demo only — core loop. Crew faces are plushies; lineup is a first pass and can be swapped. No XP, no boxes from this game.
