Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Everything lives on your device — there are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your progress. You can also reset all progress from the profile screen inside the app.

How do purchases and Premium work?

Story packs are one-time purchases; Premium is an annual subscription that unlocks all current and future packs. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. To restore purchases on a new device, use the Restore Purchases option in the app's store screen while signed in to the same store account.

What is the Plushie Cabinet?

Earn XP by completing quests and journals, then spend 100 XP to open a blind box in the Plushie Cabinet. Collected plushies can join your quests as sidekicks for bonus XP and tips.
