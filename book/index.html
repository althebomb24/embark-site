The Moonseed Garden

Book One of the Embark series

You found the garden gate! The Moonseed Garden is a gentle picture book about Pip the garden sprite, Atlas the dragon, and a young gardener who learns that small things done daily — a pat of soil, a cup of water, one more try — grow into something wonderful.

The Embark app is coming soon

The Moonseed Garden began in Embark, the app that turns real-life goals into story-driven quests. Grown-ups can visit all five of Embark's realms, earn XP, collect plushie sidekicks, and tend habits of their own — one tiny quest at a time.

Embark is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. This page will bloom into download links the day the app launches — check back soon!

More Embark Stories are growing

The Moonseed Garden is the first Embark picture book. More journeys are on the way: the Village of Alderbrook, the Starship Meridian, the Frontier Ranch, and the Detective's Agency.
