How it plays (from memory, gaps filled in):
· Each turn you get a random piece — a mystery box, Thug most often. Thug 70%, Cop 10%, Enforcer 10%, Detective 3%, Boss 4%, Car 2%, Syndicate 1%. (Cop pieces are 15% of draws.) Mob Boss is merge-only — merge three Syndicates. You must place it, robber or cop. You also have one hold slot to stash a piece for later.
· 3 or more connected same pieces merge into the next tier (Thug → Enforcer → Boss → Syndicate → Mob Boss; Cop → Detective → Car → Jail → …) on the square you last placed (or most recently created/moved, for cops). Merging frees space — without it you box yourself in.
· You do not move. Only Cops move, one square at a time in a random direction; Detectives, Cars, and higher units stay put. Pieces block them — so your placements steer them.
· Box in 1 cop by itself → Detective. 3 or more Detectives together → Cop Car. Box in 3 cops at once → Cop Car directly.
· 3 or more Cars → Jail. 3 or more Jails → Prison, and so on up the chain (Prison → Precinct → HQ). Each jail-level unit, when formed, clears robbers around it — Jail clears the 8 touching squares, Prison clears within 2, Precinct within 3, HQ clears the whole board. Clearing helps you: it frees big space.
· Score: +1 per piece placed, +2 per robber cleared. Game over when the board is full and you cannot place. Survive as long as you can.
